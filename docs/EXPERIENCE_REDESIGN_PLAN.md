# CakeWish Experience Redesign

CakeWish retained its React/TypeScript/Vite/R3F routes, existing landing, two tariffs, order handoff, WebGL fallback, ErrorBoundary and safe texture loading. Existing regressions were hard-coded stages, primitive repeated previews, fixed microphone threshold, no config timeline and public order-media risk.

The implemented architecture is `giftConfig -> typed experience.sections -> timeline hook -> intro registry/scene/finale registry`; personalization remains data-only. Tina informed the fast entrance and terminal pacing. Birthday Bloom informed cinematic copy, gallery variants and data-first configuration. Fajar informed ordered sections. VIDAKHOSHPEY22 and Tanbaycu informed explicit-action microphone/share/onboarding principles. Ascodeasice informed hold interaction principles. No external source, shader, models or media was copied.

GLB cake loading is prepared through `GiftCake`/`ModelCake` and `modelUrl`; procedural rendering remains active until Blender exports are reviewed and committed.
